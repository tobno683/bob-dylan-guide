About this guide

What it is

An independent, non-commercial fan guide, with no affiliation to Bob Dylan, his management, Columbia Records, Sony or Universal. It is a static website: no tracking, no analytics, no cookies, no accounts, no advertising.

On lyrics and images

Dylan's lyrics are under copyright and are not reproduced here beyond short fragments quoted as commentary. The complete official lyrics are at bobdylan.com. Photographs are used only where they are public domain or Creative Commons licensed, credited to the photographer and linked to the source; the style page describes the looks it cannot legally show. The header illustration is the site's own artwork.

On accuracy

Dylan's biography is unusually full of disputed facts, invented origin stories and accounts that contradict each other — several of them supplied by Dylan himself. Where the record is genuinely unclear, this guide tries to say so rather than choosing the better story. Dates come from the standard discographies and from contemporary reporting; where a date is commonly given two ways, the more widely documented one is used.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, it should be fixed. All the content lives in plain JavaScript data files under assets/data/ in the repository — one file per section, readable and editable without touching any of the page code. Open an issue or a pull request.

Colophon

Hand-built static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no dependencies. Typeset in Bodoni Moda, Spectral and Oswald. Dark by default, with a light theme a click away in the header; your choice is remembered. Press / anywhere to search every page at once.
