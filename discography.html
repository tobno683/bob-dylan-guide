The Records

Discography

All forty studio albums across sixty-four years, the major live records, every volume of the Bootleg Series, and two supergroup albums with joke surnames. Filter by kind, or search for anything.

Studio albums are numbered in release order. Dates are original release dates; almost everything here has been remastered and reissued since. The Bootleg Series filter shows 16 releases covering volumes 1–18, because Vol. 1–3 was issued as a single box. The Live and Compilation lists are selective — he has released far more of both, and the full discography runs to hundreds of entries.
