Library

More has been written about Dylan than about any other popular musician, and a good deal of it is bad. This is the part worth your time.

Where to start

One book

Chronicles: Volume One. His own, and the best-written thing anyone has produced on the subject — even where it is evasive, which is often.

One biography

Robert Shelton, No Direction Home. By the critic who discovered him, with access nobody else had. Heylin is more complete; Shelton is better company.

One film

Dont Look Back. Ninety-six minutes of 1965 with no commentary and no hindsight. Nothing else gets that close.
