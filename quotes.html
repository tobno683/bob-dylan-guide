Spoken and written Dylan, with a source attached to every line. Everything here is something he said or wrote in prose — not a song lyric.

Why no lyrics? Dylan's song lyrics are under copyright, and a fan site that reprints them is a fan site waiting to be taken down. The official lyrics live at bobdylan.com. Half the quotations misattributed to Dylan online are also lyrics he never wrote, or things he never said — everything on this page has a date and a place.
