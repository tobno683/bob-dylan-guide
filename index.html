Still on the road

The Rough and Rowdy Ways tour began in November 2021 and is still running. It passes its 300th concert in 2026 — a 27-date US run opened in Omaha on 21 March, with summer dates through the Southwest and Midwest, Forest Hills Stadium in New York on 21 July, and five nights at London's Royal Festival Hall at the end of the year. He is 85, and in his 65th year of performing.
