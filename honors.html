Honors

The only songwriter with a Nobel Prize in Literature, and the only Nobel laureate who did not turn up to collect it. Here is the rest of the shelf.

How he takes them

Badly, mostly, and that is part of the charm. He wrote a song attacking the Princeton honorary doctorate he had just accepted. He wore sunglasses for the Presidential Medal of Freedom and said nothing. He went silent for two weeks after the Nobel announcement and then skipped the ceremony. The exception is the 1991 Grammy Lifetime Achievement speech, which is short, strange and unmistakably sincere — and the 2015 MusiCares address, which is the fullest account he has ever given of his own work, delivered to a room that had just given him an award and spent thirty-five minutes listening to him settle scores.
